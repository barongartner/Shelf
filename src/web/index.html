      Share Sheet

      Put Shelf in the share sheet of every app. Share a link, a photo or a file, tap Shelf, and it lands on the shelf without leaving the app you are in.

      1. Tap Install the Shortcut. It copies your share address and downloads the Shortcut.
      2. If Shortcuts does not open by itself, open the download (Files, Downloads, Shelf).
      3. Tap Set Up Shortcut, paste into the box, and tap Add Shortcut.
      4. Try it: share a link from Safari, scroll down and tap Shelf.

      Or build it yourself:

      1. Tap Copy share address below.
      2. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +. Tap the name at the top, choose Rename and call it Shelf. In the same menu tap Details, turn on Show in Share Sheet and tap Done.
      3. Tap Search Actions, add Repeat with Each. If it does not already say Shortcut Input, tap its blue word and pick Shortcut Input.
      4. Search again and add Get Contents of URL. Drag it inside the repeat, between Repeat with Each and End Repeat.
      5. Tap the URL field and paste. Then tap Repeat Item in the bar above the keyboard so it lands at the very end, tap it, and pick Name.
      6. Tap the arrow on that action. Set Method to POST, Request Body to File, tap File and pick Repeat Item. Tap Done.
      7. Try it: share a link from Safari, scroll down and tap Shelf. Tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the share sheet to move it to the top.
